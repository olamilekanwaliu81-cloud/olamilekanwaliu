How It Works

Our Cover Design Process

A clear path from brief to print-ready cover — with your feedback shaping every stage.

1

Tell us about your book

Share your title, genre, audience and any cover ideas or references you like, plus your trim size and page count if known.

2

Concept & direction

We agree a clear visual direction — mood, imagery, colour and typography — that fits your genre and goals.

3

Design the cover

We design the front cover, and the full wrap with spine and back for print editions, built to the correct specifications.

4

Review & refine

You review the design and we refine colours, type and layout through the agreed revisions until it's right.

5

Prepare final files

We prepare print-ready wraps, the Kindle cover and any 3D mockups, checked to the platform's requirements.

6

Deliver & support

You receive your final files, and we help you understand how to upload and preview the cover on your platform.

Important: A great cover gives your book its best first impression, but no designer can guarantee sales, rankings or bestseller status. We focus on a professional, genre-appropriate cover built correctly for your platform.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions About Book Cover Design

Do you design covers from scratch?+

Yes. We design original covers built around your title, genre and audience. We can also refresh or rebuild an existing cover if you prefer.

Do I own the rights to my cover?+

Yes — 100%. You own the final cover design and files in full. Any licensed or stock elements are cleared for your commercial use, and you never share credit or ownership.

What do you need from me to start?+

Your title, subtitle and author name, your genre, a short description or blurb, any ideas or reference covers you like, and your trim size and page count if you have them.

Can you design for paperback, hardcover and Kindle?+

Yes. We can prepare a full print wrap (front, spine and back) for paperback and hardcover, plus a matching front-only cover for the Kindle eBook.

Do you include the spine and back cover?+

Yes, for print editions. A full wrap includes the front cover, spine (sized to your page count) and back cover with your blurb, author bio and barcode space.

Can you resize my cover for KDP and IngramSpark?+

Yes. KDP and IngramSpark use different wrap templates and sizes, so each needs its own correctly built file. We can prepare covers for both.

Do you provide 3D mockups?+

Yes. We can provide polished 3D mockup images of your cover for your website, social media and promotion, in addition to the print-ready files.

How many revisions do I get?+

Each project includes revisions so we refine the concept, colours and typography until the cover matches your vision. The exact number is agreed before we begin.

Can you match my genre?+

Yes. A romance, a thriller, a children's book and a business book each have different visual expectations. We design to the conventions of your genre so readers instantly understand your book.

What files will I receive?+

Typically a print-ready PDF wrap for each print edition, a high-resolution cover image for Kindle, and 3D mockups where included. Deliverables are confirmed before work begins.
