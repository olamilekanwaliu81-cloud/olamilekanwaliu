Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions About Amazon Ads & Funnels

Is my advertising budget included in your fee?+

No. Your Amazon Ads spend is paid directly to Amazon from your own account and is separate from our setup or management fee. We help you choose a sensible starting budget, but you control and fund the spend.

Do I need my own Amazon KDP and Ads account, and who owns it?+

Yes, the campaigns run in your own Amazon account so you keep full ownership, control and billing. We set them up and manage them with your permission; the account and data always remain yours.

What results can I expect from Amazon Ads?+

No one can honestly guarantee sales, a specific return on ad spend, rankings or bestseller status. Ads are a tool to increase visibility; results depend on your book, cover, price, reviews and market. We focus on setting up and optimising campaigns properly to give your book a fair chance.

How much should I budget for ads?+

It depends on your genre, goals and competition. Many authors start small to gather data, then adjust. We'll suggest a realistic starting point, but the spend and risk are yours to control.

What is a book sales funnel?+

A funnel is the simple path that turns a stranger into a reader and repeat buyer — often a free reader magnet, a landing page, an email sign-up and a short nurture sequence that points readers to your book and future releases.

Do you build the landing page and email funnel?+

Yes, where included in the scope. We can set up a reader-magnet landing page, connect it to your email tool, and write a basic welcome and nurture sequence. The exact deliverables are agreed in advance.

Which ad types and placements do you set up?+

Primarily Amazon Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands, using keyword and product targeting suited to your book and genre, within the options available in your account and region.

Is this a one-time setup or ongoing management?+

Both can be arranged. We can do a one-time setup and hand it over, or provide ongoing optimisation and reporting for an agreed period. We'll confirm which before starting.

Can you help if my ads or funnel aren't converting?+

Yes. We can review your existing campaigns, targeting, listing and funnel, identify likely issues and suggest changes. Some factors — like your cover, price or reviews — may need attention before ads can perform.

Do I keep control of my campaigns and data?+

Always. Everything runs in your account, you keep full access and ownership, and you can pause or change anything at any time.
