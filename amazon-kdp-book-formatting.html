What We Offer

Book Formatting Services for Amazon KDP

Every book has different layout requirements. A simple novel, a business guide, a workbook and an illustrated children's book cannot all be formatted in exactly the same way. Our services are tailored to your manuscript, book type and intended publishing format.

Service 01

Amazon KDP Paperback Formatting

Prepare your manuscript for a professional paperback interior. Depending on your book's requirements, formatting may include:

  • Trim size and page layout setup
  • Interior margins and gutter configuration
  • Chapter headings and section breaks
  • Page numbering and running headers or footers where appropriate
  • Font selection, typography and paragraph styling
  • Front matter and back matter formatting
  • Table of contents formatting where applicable
  • Image placement and caption styling
  • Blank-page and pagination checks
  • Print-ready PDF preparation and technical review

The goal is to produce an interior that is consistent, readable and prepared for the specifications of your intended paperback edition.

Service 02

Amazon KDP Hardcover Formatting

Hardcover books require attention to their own print specifications. Your paperback interior should not automatically be reused without checking the requirements for the hardcover edition. We can help prepare your hardcover interior by reviewing:

  • The selected trim size and applicable page requirements
  • Interior margins and gutter
  • Page count and pagination
  • Chapter and section consistency
  • Typography, spacing and image placement
  • PDF export settings and font embedding
  • Applicable KDP hardcover interior requirements

The final layout depends on the book's specifications and the publishing requirements in effect when your files are prepared.

Service 03

Kindle eBook Formatting

A Kindle eBook is different from a print book. Print layouts are designed around fixed pages, while most reflowable Kindle books adapt to the reader's screen, font size and device settings. Our Kindle formatting service can include:

  • Clean chapter and heading structure
  • Consistent paragraph styling
  • Clickable table of contents where appropriate
  • Properly structured front matter and back matter
  • Image placement and accessibility considerations where applicable
  • Removal of unnecessary print-layout artifacts
  • EPUB preparation and technical checks when included in the agreed scope

The result should be structured for a comfortable digital reading experience rather than simply reproducing a print PDF on a screen.

Service 04

  • Numbered steps and practical examples
  • Headers, footers and page references

The objective is to make your content organized, usable and visually consistent while preserving the purpose of each section.

Quality

What Makes a Professionally Formatted Book Different?

Professional book formatting is not simply about choosing an attractive font and adjusting margins. The interior must support the reading experience and work within the technical requirements of the intended publishing format.

Readability and consistency

Typography, paragraph spacing, headings and page structure should work together. Inconsistent formatting can distract readers and make a book feel unfinished.

Correct page layout

Print books require appropriate trim size, margins, gutter and pagination. These settings must be considered in relation to the book's specifications and content.

Font and image checks

Fonts should be handled appropriately for the intended output, and images checked for suitability at final placement size. Font embedding, image resolution and export settings can affect a print-ready file.

Structured digital files

Kindle books need a logical heading structure, sensible navigation and content that adapts appropriately to different screens when a reflowable format is used.

Pre-submission quality control

A file that looks good on your computer is not automatically ready for publication. Technical checks help identify issues before you upload your book.

Honest expectations

No formatting service can guarantee acceptance in every case, but careful preparation can help reduce avoidable file problems.

  • Font embedding and export settings

  • Table, heading and paragraph alignment

Kindle and EPUB checks

  • Navigation and table of contents
  • Paragraph and section formatting
  • Image placement and scaling

  • EPUB structural validation where included
  • Review of the final digital file

Final file review. Before delivery, the files are reviewed against the agreed specifications. Where applicable, we can also help you understand relevant warnings or problems identified during a publishing-platform preview. Technical validation and visual review serve different purposes, so both may be needed.

How It Works

Our Book Formatting Process

You should understand what happens to your manuscript before work begins.

1

Tell us about your book

Share your manuscript and explain what you intend to publish — genre, approximate word or page count, chosen trim size if known, intended formats and any special layout requirements.

2

Review the requirements

We assess the project scope and identify the formatting work required. Extensive images, tables, worksheets or unusual design elements are considered before confirming the scope.

3

Format the interior

Once the scope is agreed, the manuscript is formatted for the intended print or digital format — typography, layout, chapter styling, pagination, navigation and more.

4

Review & quality-check

We review the output for relevant formatting inconsistencies and technical issues within the agreed scope. Any additional work is discussed before proceeding.

5

Deliver your files

You receive the agreed deliverables — which may include a print-ready PDF, a Kindle-compatible file, an EPUB, or other specified files. We clarify deliverables before work begins.

6

Support your next step

If publishing assistance is included, we can help you understand the relevant upload and preview process for Amazon KDP, and review error messages that relate to the delivered file.

Featured Project 2 · Children's Chapter Book

Soul Sneakers: Passport to Play by CB Johnson

Services provided: Interior reformatting in Adobe InDesign; trim size conversion; cover resizing; Amazon KDP and IngramSpark publishing setup

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions About Amazon KDP Book Formatting

What is Amazon KDP book formatting?+

Amazon KDP book formatting is the process of preparing a manuscript for publication through Kindle Direct Publishing. It includes creating an appropriate interior layout for print books or structuring content for Kindle eBooks.

Can you format my paperback and hardcover editions?+

Yes, we can discuss preparing both editions. Their applicable specifications must be checked separately, even if they use the same manuscript.

Can you format my Kindle eBook and paperback together?+

Yes, both can be included in a project where the requirements and deliverables are agreed in advance. A Kindle eBook usually needs a different layout approach from a print interior.

Do you provide a print-ready PDF?+

A print-ready PDF can be included for paperback or hardcover projects, depending on the agreed scope. The file should be prepared for the relevant specifications of the intended edition.

Can you fix a KDP formatting rejection?+

We can review the error message, inspect the relevant file and help identify possible formatting-related causes. The exact solution depends on the problem. Not every rejection is caused by the interior file.

Will my book be accepted by Amazon KDP?+

No formatter can honestly guarantee acceptance in every situation. We can prepare and review files against applicable requirements, but KDP makes the final decision, and issues outside the formatting scope may affect a submission.

Do you work with first-time authors?+

Yes. First-time authors can share their manuscript, explain their publishing goals and receive guidance on the formatting requirements relevant to their project.

How much does book formatting cost?+

The cost depends on the manuscript's length, complexity, intended formats, images, tables and required deliverables. Share your project details to request a quote.

What files do I need to provide?+

A clean, editable manuscript is usually the best starting point. You can also provide your preferred trim size, reference designs, illustrations, tables, existing cover specifications and any relevant KDP error messages. If you don't know the technical specifications yet, explain your publishing goals so the requirements can be discussed before work begins.

Can you help me publish my book on Amazon?+

Publishing assistance can be discussed as part of a separate or combined project. The exact services included, account responsibilities and deliverables should be agreed before work starts.
