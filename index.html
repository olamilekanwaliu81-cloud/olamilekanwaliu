Questions & Answers

Everything You Want to Know

The details authors ask most, answered up front — so you can reach out with confidence.

How long does each package take?+

It depends on scope. Formatting and cover projects typically take 3–7 days. Full ghostwriting and publishing packages run 3–8 weeks. You'll get a clear, realistic timeline during your free discovery call before any commitment.

What do you need from me to get started?+

Just your manuscript or idea, any notes or references you have, and a short brief on your goals and audience. If you're starting from scratch, we'll map it out together on the discovery call.

Do I own full rights to the finished book?+

Yes — 100%. You are the sole author and copyright owner of every manuscript, cover, and file I deliver. I never claim credit or reuse your work anywhere.

Will my name be the only author listed?+

Absolutely. Ghostwriting is fully confidential — your name is the only one on the book. I remain completely behind the scenes.

What if I'm not happy with a draft?+

Every package includes revisions so we refine the work until it matches your vision. If we haven't started work yet, you're entitled to a full refund. Nobody gets left with something they don't love.

What file formats do I receive?+

You get the formats you need to publish and share: editable Word (.docx), print-ready PDF, and ePub for eBooks — all delivered ready for Amazon KDP and IngramSpark.

Do you help if KDP or IngramSpark rejects my file?+

Yes. If a platform rejects a file I formatted, I fix it and re-submit at no extra cost. You're never left troubleshooting uploads on your own.

Can I pick up a partially finished manuscript?+

Definitely. Whether you have three chapters or a rough outline, I can step in mid-project, match your voice, and carry it through to a finished, published book.

Do you sign an NDA and keep my story confidential?+

Yes. Your story stays private, and I'm happy to sign an NDA before we begin so your ideas are protected in writing from day one.

What currencies and payment methods do you accept?+

All currencies are accepted. Prices are shown in USD, but you can pay the equivalent amount in your local currency (NGN, GBP, EUR, CAD and more) through Fiverr's secure checkout or by direct arrangement.

Can I see samples before I commit?+

Of course. Browse the portfolio section above for real book covers, interior layouts, and KDP projects — and I'm happy to share more relevant samples on your discovery call.

Is the discovery call free, and am I obligated to anything?+

The discovery call is completely free with zero obligation. It's simply a chance to talk through your book, get honest advice, and see if we're a good fit.
