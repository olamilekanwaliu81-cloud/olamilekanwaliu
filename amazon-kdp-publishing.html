Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions About Amazon KDP Publishing

Do you publish the book for me, or just prepare the files?+

Both can be arranged. We can prepare your print-ready and eBook files and, when included in the scope, guide or carry out the Amazon KDP upload, metadata setup and preview checks so your book goes live correctly.

Do I need my own Amazon KDP account, and who owns it?+

You should own your KDP account so you keep full control, rights and royalties. We can help you set it up in your name, or work within your existing account with your permission. The account and book always remain yours.

Do I need an ISBN?+

Kindle eBooks don't require an ISBN. For paperback and hardcover, Amazon can provide a free KDP ISBN, or you can use your own. We'll explain the options so you can choose what's right for your goals.

Can you publish paperback, hardcover and Kindle together?+

Yes. These can be published and linked on the same Amazon listing, with the correct file and specification for each format, where included in the agreed scope.

How long does publishing take once files are ready?+

Uploading and setting up a listing is usually quick, but Amazon's own review before a book goes live typically takes up to 72 hours. We'll give you a realistic timeline for your project.

How do royalties and pricing work?+

Royalties are paid by Amazon directly to you based on your list price and royalty option. We can help you set a sensible price and the right royalty plan, but actual earnings depend on sales.

Will my book rank or sell well after publishing?+

No one can honestly guarantee sales, rankings or bestseller status. We focus on publishing your book correctly with optimised metadata and positioning to give it the best fair chance; results depend on the market and your marketing.

Can you publish to other platforms besides Amazon?+

Yes. In addition to Amazon KDP, we can help you reach IngramSpark, Apple Books, Kobo, Barnes & Noble and more, depending on your chosen distribution strategy and the agreed scope.

What if Amazon KDP rejects or flags my book?+

We can review the message, inspect the relevant file or metadata and help identify likely causes. Amazon makes the final decision, and not every issue is caused by the files, but we'll help resolve what we can.

Do I keep full rights and control of my book?+

Always. You remain the sole author, rights holder and account owner. We never claim credit, ownership or control of your book.
